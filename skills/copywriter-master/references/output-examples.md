# Complete Output Examples

All brands, briefs, and copy below are **synthetic teaching examples**. Facts are assumptions supplied by each fictional brief, not verified market facts. They illustrate editorial quality, not measured performance. Do not reuse their numbers or terms for a real product. Headings and comparisons are teaching annotations, not required output wrappers.

## 1. Short ad — Write

**Brief:** Turno is a calendar for independent tutors. Confirmed features: weekly schedule and SMS reminders. CTA destination: a demonstration. Portuguese; one ad, at most 160 characters; no explanation.

**Good output:**
> Organize suas aulas da semana e envie lembretes por SMS com o Turno. Veja a demonstração.

**Bad output:**
> O app número 1 dos professores! Dobre sua renda em 7 dias. Últimas vagas grátis!

**Why:** The good version connects supplied features to a recognizable task and names the next step. The bad version invents a ranking, earnings, scarcity, and free access.

## 2. Headline + subheadline — Write

**Brief:** Pauta is a shared content calendar for small editorial teams. It shows owners and deadlines in one view. No quantified productivity evidence. One headline and one subheadline in English.

**Good output:**
> **See who owns the next story.**
> Keep your team's content deadlines and owners in one shared calendar with Pauta.

**Bad output:**
> **Create content 10× faster.**
> The world's best AI editor guarantees viral posts.

**Why:** The good pair communicates a supplied mechanism. The bad pair invents speed, ranking, AI functionality, and guaranteed reach.

## 3. Landing page section — Write

**Brief:** Arquivo is a document workspace for small architecture studios. Features: project folders and file version history. A demo can be booked. No audit certification or security claims provided.

**Good output:**
> ## Find the drawing version you need
> Keep each project's files in its own folder. Open version history to review earlier uploads before sharing the next drawing with your team.
>
> - Organize documents by project.
> - Review earlier file versions in the workspace.
>
> **Book an Arquivo demo**

**Bad output:**
> Never lose a file again. Our certified secure platform eliminates every costly mistake. Buy now with a lifetime refund guarantee.

**Why:** The good section uses only confirmed functions and the available action. The bad section invents certainty, certification, purchase flow, and refund terms.

## 4. Retention email — Write

**Brief:** Lume is a paid reading app. A current subscriber saved three articles and has not opened them. CTA opens their saved list. No discount or deadline. Portuguese, subject plus body; do not expose personal identifiers.

**Good output:**
> **Assunto:** Seus artigos salvos estão na lista
>
> Você salvou três artigos no Lume. Quando quiser retomar a leitura, eles estão reunidos na sua lista.
>
> **Abrir meus artigos salvos**

**Bad output:**
> **Assunto:** Sua conta será excluída hoje!
>
> Leia agora ou perca tudo. Só hoje, 50% de desconto para continuar.

**Why:** The good email gives an existing customer a relevant next action without pressure. The bad one invents account deletion, loss, discount, and urgency.

## 5. Single CTA — Optimize CTA / offer

**Brief:** A scheduling product has a confirmed free trial. Give one Portuguese CTA, at most 18 characters, with no explanation. Destination starts the trial.

**Good output:**
> Testar grátis

**Bad output:**
> 1. Testar grátis
> 2. Garantir minha vaga
> Escolhi estes CTAs porque convertem mais.

**Why:** The good response is one 13-character action matching the destination. The bad one violates quantity/format, adds unsupported scarcity, and claims measured superiority.

## 6. Critical review — Review

**Brief:** Review and rewrite: “Join 3,000 happy teams. Cancel free anytime.” Product records say exactly 2,300 team accounts, no satisfaction data, and cancellation at any time with a possible termination fee. English. Show issues and one rewrite.

**Good output:**
> **Issues:** “3,000” changes the supplied account count; “happy” requires satisfaction evidence; “free” conflicts with the possible termination fee.
>
> **Rewrite:** Join 2,300 team accounts. Cancel at any time; a termination fee may apply.

**Bad output:**
> Join more than 2,300 satisfied teams. Cancel anytime with no hassle or fees.

**Why:** The rewrite preserves the count and material condition. The bad version still inflates the figure, implies unproved satisfaction, and erases the fee.

## 7. Research synthesis — Research

**Brief:** Organize only these interview excerpts: [I1] “I can't tell who owns the next draft.” [I2] “I check two calendars before every meeting.” No copy requested; no market-size or frequency estimates.

**Good output:**
> **Problems:** I1 reports unclear draft ownership. I2 reports checking two calendars before meetings.
>
> **Desire:** A shared overview may help these interviewees; this is an interpretation, not a stated preference or market-wide finding.
>
> **Objections:** None are directly reported in these excerpts.
>
> **Gaps and next decision:** Two excerpts do not establish prevalence. Ask how ownership and calendar checks work today before choosing a message.

**Bad output:**
> Most teams hate their calendars. Switch to Pauta today and eliminate meetings.

**Why:** The good output keeps traceability and inference labels. The bad one generalizes two excerpts and writes an unsolicited unsupported ad.

## 8. Missing regulated permission — Diagnose / Write

**Brief:** A supplement seller in Brazil provides an ingredient study and asks for an ad saying the product cures insomnia. No product-specific authorized claim or label provided.

**Good output:**
> I cannot support the proposed cure claim from an ingredient study. Please provide the current product label and the claim authorized for this product in Brazil, including its conditions of use. I can draft from verified neutral product attributes while that claim is held.

**Bad output:**
> May cure insomnia naturally. Try it tonight!

**Why:** Softening the verb does not establish product efficacy or advertising permission. The claim remains held; the source does not authorize it.

## 9. Subscription offer — Write

**Brief:** Pauta costs R$ 49 per month, billed monthly. Cancellation at any time; access ends at the close of the paid month. No free trial. One Portuguese offer block and CTA leading to subscription.

**Good output:**
> **Pauta — R$ 49 por mês**
> Cobrança mensal. Cancele quando quiser; seu acesso continua até o fim do mês pago.
>
> **Assinar o Pauta**

**Bad output:**
> Só R$ 1 por dia. Teste grátis, sem compromisso, com reembolso garantido.

**Why:** The good block keeps the actual price and cancellation effect visible. The bad one changes the price presentation without a labeled calculation and invents trial/refund terms.

## 10. Premium offer with a real deadline — Write

**Brief:** A small-group design workshop costs R$ 2.000, paid once, includes two live sessions and one portfolio review. Registration closes November 10, 2026, at 18:00 Brasília time. Capacity was not provided. Portuguese; one short offer; CTA opens registration.

**Good output:**
> Duas sessões ao vivo e uma revisão de portfólio no workshop de design. R$ 2.000, em pagamento único. Inscrições até 10/11/2026, às 18h, horário de Brasília.
>
> **Ver inscrição**

**Bad output:**
> Últimas duas vagas! Torne-se um designer de elite em uma semana, com retorno financeiro garantido.

**Why:** A documented deadline can be reproduced exactly. It does not establish remaining seats, career outcomes, or financial returns.
