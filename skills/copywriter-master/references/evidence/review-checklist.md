# Evidence-based review checklist

Use when reviewing or auditing a draft. It feeds the existing eight rubric dimensions and does not change their scoring: [quality-rubric.md](../quality-rubric.md) remains the scoring contract, and its hard failures still block a draft. A finding here is a reason to revise, not a score by itself.

| Check | Ask | Dimension it informs | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain wording | Did needless jargon, inflated words or artificial passive voice survive? Is each technical term required by the audience? | Readability | [Needless complexity](language-and-clarity.md) |
| Depth fits the reader | Is the explanation level right for a beginner versus a specialist, per the brief? | Audience fit | [Prior knowledge](language-and-clarity.md) |
| Concreteness | Does each abstract claim carry a checkable anchor taken from the brief? | Offer clarity, readability | [Concreteness](language-and-clarity.md) |
| One idea per block | Does each paragraph carry one idea, and does each shift in topic have a connective? | Readability | [Cognitive load](cognition-and-structure.md), [Coherence](cognition-and-structure.md) |
| Curiosity delivered | If the opening opens a gap, does the body close it? | Emotional relevance, evidence support | [Information gap](attention-and-emotion.md) |
| Credibility | Is each credential verifiable and tied to the reader's outcome, not to self-praise? | Evidence support | [Source credibility](persuasion-and-credibility.md) |
| Social proof | Is the proof real, sourced, from comparable people, and free of normalizing the unwanted behavior? | Evidence support | [Norms](persuasion-and-credibility.md) |
| Loss framing | Is any cost of inaction a supported fact rather than alarm? | Emotional relevance, compliance | [Framing](decision-and-pricing.md) |
| Price reframing | Do total price, cadence and cancellation terms stay visible beside any per-period figure or reference price? | Offer clarity, compliance | [Mental accounting](decision-and-pricing.md), [Anchors](decision-and-pricing.md) |
| Autonomy | Does the CTA stay clear and firm while leaving the choice with the reader? | CTA quality | [Reactance](resistance-and-barriers.md) |
| Belief correction | Does it respect the reader's earlier belief and give a sourced reason to change? | Audience fit | [Confirmation bias](resistance-and-barriers.md) |
| First step | Is any micro-commitment useful and honestly described, not a pointless hurdle? | CTA quality | [Foot-in-the-door](resistance-and-barriers.md) |
| Story | Is every story true, sourced and approved? | Evidence support | [Transportation](narrative-and-familiarity.md) |
| Justification | Is the strategy explained by a barrier, with a test where the choice rests on a hypothesis? | Channel fit, audience fit | [Effect sizes](effect-sizes-and-testing.md) |

## Brazilian compliance prompts

These prompts come from a supplied checklist. They are not legal advice or a current legal opinion; follow [jurisdictions/brazil.md](../jurisdictions/brazil.md) and confirm with the responsible reviewer.

- **Misleading or abusive advertising (CDC art. 37).** Free of promises of fast gains, unproven cures or unrealistic guarantees?
- **Self-regulation (CONAR).** Are the required disclaimers and risk notices present for financial, medical, gambling or similar products?
- **Originality and copyright (Lei 9.610/98).** Is the expression original, without literal copying of competitor copy or other protected material?
