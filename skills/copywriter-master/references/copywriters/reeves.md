# Rosser Reeves — Unique Selling Proposition

**Key book:** *Reality in Advertising* (1961). USP is a clear, relevant, distinctive reason to buy.

- State one proposal: buy this product and receive this benefit. It should be meaningfully distinct and strong enough to matter to the intended audience. A unique but irrelevant feature is not a persuasive difference.
- Consult for positioning and headlines in crowded categories.
- Write the audience, benefit, and reason in one sentence. Ask whether a competitor could say the same, then verify the claim is demonstrable.
- A USP must be true; proprietary wording alone is not an advantage. Repetition-heavy historical style is not a requirement.
