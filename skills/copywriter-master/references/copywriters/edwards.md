# Ray Edwards — PASTOR

Ray Edwards is a framework author, not listed among the guide's historical copywriters. PASTOR is commonly presented as Person, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response; some explanations expand substeps such as problem, pain, solution, and testimony. Check the source before teaching a specific variant.

1. **Person:** identify the reader and relevant problem. 2. **Amplify:** explain plausible consequences without dramatizing. 3. **Story:** introduce context leading to a solution. 4. **Transformation:** show a possible outcome and available proof. 5. **Offer:** state offer and terms. 6. **Response:** give a clear CTA.

Consult for long-form copy to problem-aware audiences. Amplification is not fearmongering; the framework does not guarantee performance.
