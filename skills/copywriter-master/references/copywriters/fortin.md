# Michel Fortin — QUEST

QUEST is Qualify → Understand → Educate → Stimulate → Transition.

1. Clarify who the message is for. 2. Show understanding of the problem. 3. Teach something useful. 4. Introduce interest in the solution. 5. Guide the reader to the next step.

Consult for content that educates before selling and for audiences with low awareness or comparing options. Education does not replace evidence or a clear CTA.

Source: [Fortin's explanation](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/take-users-quest-michel-fortin).
