# Claude C. Hopkins — Measurable Advertising

**Key book:** *Scientific Advertising* (1923). The editorial lens treats advertising as a measurable response process.

- Define a trackable behavior and use a reliable identifier or experiment. Prefer specific facts and details to generic claims. Offer a low-risk way to try a product only when it exists.
- Consult when a piece can be measured, for direct-response ads, or for real samples and trials.
- Set an outcome and guardrail, write a hypothesis tied to an observed barrier, and track each variation consistently. A test applies to its context, not every future campaign.
- A pre-emptive claim must be true, verifiable, and relevant; confirm historical attribution before citing it. Do not imply that a common category feature is proprietary.
