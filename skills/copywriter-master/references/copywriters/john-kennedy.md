# John E. Kennedy — “Salesmanship in Print” and Reason-Why

Kennedy's work is associated with Lord & Thomas and Albert Lasker around 1904; accounts differ. His principles appeared in articles and were collected in *Reason Why Advertising*.

- Replace an empty assertion with a concrete, verifiable reason to believe. Ask what reason the reader has to act and what a salesperson would say in response to a real question.
- Consult for skeptical audiences, complex offers, and copy that sounds attractive but does not persuade.
- State the promise, add a factual reason or mechanism, and remove lines that do not help the decision. A reason is not proof if it is only proprietary language.

Source: [Advertising Hall of Fame](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/john-e-kennedy/).
