# John Caples — Headlines and Testing

**Work:** the piano-course ad associated with Caples appeared around 1925–1926; dates vary. **Key book:** *Tested Advertising Methods* (1932; later editions).

- Explore self-interest, news, and curiosity. Be specific and reader-centered. A curiosity hook must be answered in the body. Keep headlines understandable on their own.
- Consult for headlines, subject lines, and response-copy testing. Vary the appeal, not only the wording.
- Explore distinct appeals in the quantity the user requested. Discard vague or unsupported options. Propose a test only when useful and feasible.
- CTR alone does not prove business impact; define a primary metric and guardrails.

Sources: [Smithsonian NMAH archive](https://sirismm.si.edu/EADpdfs/NMAH.AC.0393.pdf), [Advertising Hall of Fame](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/john-caples/), [Creative Hall of Fame](https://creativehalloffame.org/inductees/john-caples/).
