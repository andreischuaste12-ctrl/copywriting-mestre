# Russell Colley — DAGMAR

DAGMAR (*Defining Advertising Goals for Measured Advertising Results*, 1961) is a communication-planning model, not a copywriting formula.

- Define an observable communication objective such as awareness, understanding, conviction, or action, with a metric and timeframe. Evaluate against that objective, not clicks alone.
- Consult for campaign briefs, primary metrics, guardrails, and learning reports.
- Use DAGMAR to plan and measure; do not treat it as a required paragraph sequence.
