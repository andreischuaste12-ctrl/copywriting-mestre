# Lester Wunderman — Direct Marketing and Relationships

Associated with direct marketing, *Being Direct*, and Columbia's mail-order record club. Sources differ on the date the term “direct marketing” became common. The credit for introducing the toll-free number comes from secondary sources and is not treated here as established fact.

- Make response identifiable and traceable. Plan for the customer relationship, including repeat purchase and retention, not just the first transaction. Make the response path simple.
- Consult for CRM, relationship email, loyalty, subscriptions, and post-purchase sequences.
- Define the response event and identifier, write for a specific segment, and plan the next step. Collect only necessary personal data with appropriate transparency and lawful basis.

Source: [Advertising Hall of Fame](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/lester-wunderman/).
