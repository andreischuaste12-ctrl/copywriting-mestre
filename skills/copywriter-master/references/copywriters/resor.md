# Helen Lansdowne Resor — Emotional and Sensory Appeal

Associated with consumer advertising at J. Walter Thompson and Woodbury's Facial Soap. The editorial lens connects emotional benefit with creative leadership.

- Anchor an emotional promise in a tangible benefit; address the actual audience in language relevant to its aspirations. Coordinate image, copy, and offer.
- Consult for beauty, home, family, and personal-care products where sensory or emotional benefits matter.
- Name a relevant emotion, connect it to a concrete product feature, and state a simple next step.
- Avoid stereotypes and guaranteed appearance claims. Attribute campaigns to the team and client.
