# David Ogilvy — Research, Facts, and Clarity

**Key books:** *Confessions of an Advertising Man* (1963), *Ogilvy on Advertising* (1983). The Rolls-Royce campaign is commonly associated with factual headlines.

- Research the product and audience. A relevant, specific fact can strengthen a headline. Use longer copy when it helps a considered decision, and organize it clearly.
- Consult for considered purchases, B2B, premium categories, product pages, and evidence-led ads.
- List up to five confirmed product facts; use fewer when fewer are available. Choose the fact most relevant to the audience and channel, then support it in the body.
- Treat performance figures reported by an agency as self-reported unless independently verified. Credit the campaign team and client.
