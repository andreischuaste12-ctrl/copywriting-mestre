# John E. Powers — Candid Retail Copy

Powers is associated with Wanamaker's department store in the late 1870s or 1880; dates differ. He is often described as an early full-time advertising copywriter.

- Say what a product is, does, and costs in plain, factual language. Replace superlatives with verifiable details. Acknowledge real limitations when useful.
- Consult for retail product pages, catalogs, and skeptical audiences.
- List up to three confirmed facts; use only those available. Draft without adjectives, then add a relevant benefit.
- Candor need not sound cold. The historical record is limited, so use this as an editorial lens rather than attributing invented lines to Powers.

Source: [Advertising Hall of Fame](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/john-e-powers/).
