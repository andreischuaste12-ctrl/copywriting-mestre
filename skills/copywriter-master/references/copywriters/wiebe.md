# Joanna Wiebe — Research and Digital Copy

Associated with conversion copywriting and Copyhackers. The editorial lens combines customer-language research, message clarity, and testable hypotheses.

- Review interviews, reviews, support tickets, and surveys for audience language. Use actual customer wording rather than internal company terms. Connect test hypotheses to observed barriers.
- Consult for landing pages, product pages, onboarding, SaaS, and ecommerce when VoC material exists.
- Extract up to ten relevant literal phrases, or fewer when fewer are available. Never invent, duplicate, or rewrite a quote. Count themes only when source and sample support a frequency claim. Without VoC, request sources or label wording as a hypothesis.
- One quote does not represent a market; protect personal data and obtain permission before advertising with testimonials. Review mining is a research method, not proof of conversion lift.
