# Eugene Schwartz — Desire, Awareness, and Sophistication

**Key book:** *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966). The editorial lens is to channel existing market desire and match the message opening to audience awareness and category sophistication.

| Awareness hypothesis | Possible entry point |
|---|---|
| Unaware | Recognizable scene, insight, or story |
| Problem-aware | Name the problem and real consequence |
| Solution-aware | Explain solution type and relevant mechanism |
| Product-aware | Proof, objections, and terms |
| Most-aware | Offer, price, deadline if real, and CTA |

Assess repeated category promises; greater familiarity may call for clearer differentiation and proof, not a larger unsupported promise. Consult during audience diagnosis. Awareness levels are a working map, not a validated psychological scale; one person may vary by category. Do not claim a mechanism is unique unless it is.
