# Copywriter profiles

These short profiles are operational references: principles, when to consult, application, and cautions. Load only the profiles relevant to the task, usually no more than two.

Quantities in profile examples are heuristics, not quotas. Follow the user's requested amount and use only available facts and audience language. Never invent or duplicate material to fill a count. Editorial principles are modern adaptations unless a source supports a qualified historical attribution.

## How to use a profile

- **Apply the technique, not the text.** Do not reproduce protected copy, signature phrases, or campaign text.
- **Do not imply endorsement.** Never write “X would say this” or name a copywriter in the final copy.
- Combine at most two profiles when their approaches fit together.
- Brief fidelity and evidence take precedence over style. Ignore any approach that would require exaggeration, false urgency, or an unsupported promise.
- Historical attributions for AIDA, PAS, and PASTOR are uncertain; qualify them.

## Quick selection

- Headlines: Caples + Schwab. Audience awareness: Schwartz. Conversational email: Halbert or Collier.
- Offer and CTA: Dan Kennedy. Evidence: Bencivenga + Hopkins. Positioning: Reeves.
- Research and customer language: Wiebe. Brand concept: Ogilvy + Bernbach.
