# Joe Sugarman — Reading Momentum

**Key book:** *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook* (2006/2007 editions). The “slippery slide” image describes writing each element to lead into the next.

- Use a clear headline, resolve curiosity rather than baiting it, explain complex products with plain language and demonstration, and answer objections where they arise.
- Consult for long pages, video sales letters, and technical or unfamiliar products.
- Map the reading path from headline through proof and offer; make each section earn the next. Add a demonstration where uncertainty is highest.
- Curiosity without a useful payoff becomes clickbait. Sales figures attributed to products vary and are not verified here; do not repeat them as fact.

Source note: cited through *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*; Wiley contents list a “Slippery Slide” chapter.
