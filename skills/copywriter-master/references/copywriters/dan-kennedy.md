# Dan Kennedy — Offer and Direct Marketing

**Associated book:** *The Ultimate Sales Letter* (1990). The practical lens is a clear offer, trackable response, and structured sales message.

- Clarify composition, price, terms, bonus, guarantee, and deadline. Ask for action directly; give a reason to act now only when a real deadline or limit exists.
- Consult for sales letters, sales email, service offers, launches, and CTAs.
- Summarize the offer, write the CTA and any relevant postscript, then define how response will be measured.
- Bonuses and scarcity do not make an offer good by themselves. Inventory and deadlines must be real.
