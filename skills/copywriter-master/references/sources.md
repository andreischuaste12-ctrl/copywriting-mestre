## Attribution and evidence rules

- Label sales, circulation, or campaign-effect figures from an author or agency as self-reported unless independently verified.
- Campaigns are team work involving writing, art, research, media, and client contributions.
- Several dates and popular attributions remain disputed. Check the original work before presenting a specific attribution as fact.
- Links added from the supplied audit are explicitly marked as not opened live in this review. Do not imply independent verification.
