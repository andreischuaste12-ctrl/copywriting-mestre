# Briefing dimensions

A checklist for deciding what is still unknown about a commercial request. It does not make every project a twelve-question interview. Fill each dimension from the request, the conversation and the project files first, then ask only about gaps that would change the message or block accurate work.

| # | Dimension | What to establish | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Objective and exact action | The single action expected from the reader | `cta.action`, accepted CTA phrases |
| 2 | Audience and context | Who decides, and their situation, habits and expertise | `audience` |
| 3 | Awareness level | Unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware or most aware (a hypothesis) | Conversation |
| 4 | Concrete problem | The real situation that causes frustration now | Conversation |
| 5 | Offer and mechanism | Product, price, terms and what competitors do not provide | `offer.name`, `offer.facts` |
| 6 | Proof and assets | Testimonials, data, certifications, cases and auditable numbers available | `claims`, `sources` |
| 7 | Primary objection | The main reason the reader does not act now | Conversation |
| 8 | Channel and format | Where it appears, limits, quantity | `format`, `constraints` |
| 9 | Tone of voice | Technical, close, provocative, corporate or inspirational | Conversation |
| 10 | Guarantee and risk reversal | Exact wording of any guarantee, trial or refund | `offer.facts` (verbatim) |
| 11 | Legal and ethical limits | Category, market and claims needing permission | `category`, claim authorization |
| 12 | Success metric and test | How results will be measured and which hypothesis is tested | Conversation |

## How to use it

- Ask a concise, prioritized set of questions as SKILL.md describes: normally up to three per message, combining related points. When the user explicitly invites a full strategic discovery, ask more in one organized message, still highest impact first.
- Dimensions 3, 4, 7, 9 and 12 are not fields of the structured brief, which is strict by design. Keep them in the conversation, not in `brief.json`.
- Never fill a gap with an invented price, proof, guarantee or permission. State a low-risk assumption and label it; hold what depends on a missing fact.
- Dimension 10 and any numbers are reproduced exactly as supplied.
- Link the answers to evidence through the barrier: [evidence/principles.md](evidence/principles.md).
