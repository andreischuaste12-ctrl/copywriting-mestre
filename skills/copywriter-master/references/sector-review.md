# Sector and market review templates

Use these prompts to structure human review. They are not a legal rules database or authorization. Check current official sources, product facts and channel policy for the precise market. Never infer legal permission from a study, a source link, or a passing local validator. Do not use these templates to add claims not requested by the user.

| Sector | Confirm before copy | Typical claim/term checks | Useful final-copy formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS / B2B | Actual feature, compatibility, billing cadence, trial/cancellation conditions | Savings and productivity need scoped evidence; terms stay visible | Landing page, pricing offer, demo script |
| E-commerce | Exact item, total price, shipping/tax scope, delivery/returns, real inventory | No invented scarcity, certification, discount reference or delivery guarantee | Social ad, product page, checkout offer |
| Education | Curriculum, workload, qualification, price and access | Completion is not job placement; distinguish accreditation from attendance | Landing page, email sequence, course offer |
| Fintech / finance | Product/category, market, applicable permissions, costs and risks | No guaranteed returns without valid basis/permission; a bookkeeping tool is not an investment | Offer, product page, onboarding explanation |
| Health / supplements | Current label, product-specific allowed wording, category and conditions | An ingredient study does not authorize a cure claim; hold pending benefits | Neutral product facts, label CTA, reviewed ad |
| Services | Service area, actual availability, price/scope, booking action and terms | Availability is not fake scarcity; quote is not a confirmed appointment | SMS CTA, booking page, sales script |

For email sequences and funnels, review each step and the overall promise: the ad, landing page, pricing and CTA destination must agree. Do not let a short ad make a broad promise that conditions later reverse. In SMS, count the actual platform's encoding/segment limits separately; the validator's NFC code-point limit is not an SMS billing-segment calculator.

## Market worksheet

Record country/member state, audience age, product category, exact claim, supporting source, authorization/label reference if required, date checked, channel conditions, reviewer and unresolved questions. Evidence and permission are separate columns. Missing jurisdiction or applicable permission means hold the dependent claim, not infer clearance.

| Market | Review questions | Official starting points (not checked for current permission in this release) |
|---|---|---|
| Brazil | CDC advertising/offer rules; CONAR guidance; LGPD and relevant sector rules; current product-specific wording | [ANVISA](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/), [CVM](https://www.gov.br/cvm/), [ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd/); see [Brazil guidance](brazilian-compliance.md) |
| United States | FTC truthfulness/substantiation; applicable federal/state and sector rules; endorsements and material disclosures | [FTC advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing), [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/) |
| European Union | Applicable member-state requirements, unfair commercial practices, GDPR, category/label restrictions and channel rules | [Unfair Commercial Practices Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2005/29/oj), [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) |

Politics, gambling, child-directed offers and other regulated categories require their own current specialist review; the general templates do not authorize those campaigns. A reviewer should record what remains unconfirmed instead of using a universal regional “safe” flag.
