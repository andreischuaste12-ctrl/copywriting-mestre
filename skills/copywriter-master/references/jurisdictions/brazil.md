# Brazilian Advertising and Data-Protection Notes

This is an editorial checklist, not legal advice or a current legal opinion. Confirm the applicable law, official guidance, product category, market, channel rules, and facts with the responsible compliance or legal team before publication.

## Separate proof from permission

For every material claim, answer two questions:

1. **Evidence:** What source supports the factual statement, in this product and context?
2. **Authorization:** Is that claim allowed for this product, category, market, audience, and channel under current rules?

Evidence does not create permission. Removing the word “guaranteed” or weakening a prohibited claim does not automatically make it permissible. If authorization is unknown, hold the claim and request the label, official basis, or review. Keep to neutral product attributes that are independently verifiable.

## Consumer advertising

When Brazil applies, review the Brazilian Consumer Defense Code (CDC), applicable self-regulatory guidance from CONAR, and current sector rules. The advertisement must not mislead by statement or omission. Support objective claims, present material terms clearly, and avoid hiding costs or restrictions where the consumer decides. Do not treat this checklist as a substitute for checking the current official text and applicable interpretation.

## Health, food, and supplements

For food and dietary-supplement copy, identify the exact product, ingredient, approved claim, amount, conditions of use, and required warning or label language. Check current Anvisa rules and the actual product labeling. Do not generalize from an ingredient study to the finished product or imply diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or guaranteed results without an authorized basis.

## Privacy and testimonials

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), determine the lawful basis and purpose for personal-data processing, data minimization, transparency, retention, access controls, and data-subject rights. Consent is not the only possible legal basis; do not invent one. Distinguish withdrawal of consent from objection where the legal basis and context differ. Do not expose personal data from reviews, interviews, or support tickets without appropriate authorization and safeguards. An authentic customer statement still needs permission and context before use as an advertisement.

## Before release

- Verify exact wording, evidence, audience, eligibility, and material conditions.
- Confirm local permission separately from proof.
- Show total price, recurring charges, cancellation terms, deadlines, and exclusions clearly.
- Use only genuine endorsements and disclose material relationships when required.
- Check current platform policies as well as law; platform approval is not legal approval.
- Escalate uncertainty to the responsible legal or regulatory reviewer.
